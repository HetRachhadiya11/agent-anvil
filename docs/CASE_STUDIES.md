## Shubh Lam: B2B catalogue and staff workflows

**Role:** voluntary website and portal development, 2026.

The project combines a public B2B catalogue with staff workflows for recording
sales visits and following up with customers. The public site and the staff
portal serve different audiences and access requirements.

My contribution includes:

- Translating business requirements into catalogue navigation and enquiry flows.
- Developing visit records, follow-up workflows and reporting filters.
- Supporting location capture and separate staff and administrator access.
- Testing workflows and refining behaviour through user feedback.

The engineering focus is on making everyday work easy to record and retrieve,
with access controls suited to each user's role. Development combines
AI-assisted implementation with workflow design, testing and iteration.

**Explore:** [public website](https://shubhlam.com/) ·
[role recorded on ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0008-9577-2906).

The public website demonstrates the catalogue. Staff workflows require
authorised access; the link is not a public portal demonstration. This
repository does not distribute Shubh Lam's application source.

## Curo Group: business intelligence and data quality

**Role:** Data Insight Analyst, 2025–present.

My work connects operational questions to SQL logic, Power BI models and
reports that stakeholders can examine and use. It spans governance, HR and
operations, with an emphasis on definitions, reconciliation and traceability.

| Work area | My contribution | Engineering focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central Action Tracker | Views of action owners, deadlines, status and ageing, with summary-to-detail navigation and stakeholder-led refinements | Consistent status definitions, date logic and usable filters |
| Risk and governance reporting | Risk-calculation validation, reconciliation of reporting methods and explanations supporting committee reporting | Traceable calculations and agreement between source data and reported measures |
| Data protection reporting | Reporting on subject access requests and data incidents | Workflow states, deadline logic, distinct counts and data-quality checks |
| People and training analytics | People First API investigation, department mappings, training reporting and technical documentation | Join keys, missing records, integration coverage and clear data requirements |

**Tools:** SQL/T-SQL, Python, Power BI, DAX, Power Query and REST/OData APIs.

These are personal contribution summaries. Employer systems and datasets
